# Firmware 0.7.2 acceleration and timing evidence

Source: [SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP v0.7.2, commit c12c475](https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP/tree/c12c475fe067e74005e7dd83999bc3a19b478b15). The observed firmware version string does not prove that a device runs an unmodified build.

For the LSM6DSV SoftFusion path, firmware [scales and calibrates accelerometer samples](https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP/blob/c12c475fe067e74005e7dd83999bc3a19b478b15/src/sensors/softfusion/SoftfusionCalibration.h#L199-L218) using [gravity divided by sensor sensitivity](https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP/blob/c12c475fe067e74005e7dd83999bc3a19b478b15/src/sensors/softfusion/imuconsts.h#L45-L49). [SensorFusion](https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP/blob/c12c475fe067e74005e7dd83999bc3a19b478b15/src/sensors/SensorFusion.cpp#L81-L125) derives gravity in the sensor coordinate frame from its orientation estimate and subtracts 9.80665 m/s². The transmitted vector is therefore firmware-estimated **gravity-removed acceleration in sensor axes**, not raw accelerometer readings, for this specific path. It depends on calibration and on the gravity/orientation estimate. The firmware [applies `sensorOffset` to the sent quaternion but sends the acceleration separately](https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP/blob/c12c475fe067e74005e7dd83999bc3a19b478b15/src/sensors/sensor.cpp#L35-L72), so the orientation and acceleration frames must not be assumed identical without verifying the mounting/offset convention.

The LSM6DSV driver specifies nominal [120 Hz acceleration and 240 Hz gyroscope rates](https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP/blob/c12c475fe067e74005e7dd83999bc3a19b478b15/src/sensors/softfusion/drivers/lsm6dsv.h#L44-L50). It consumes tagged FIFO samples, and SoftFusion [uses calibrated sample intervals internally](https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP/blob/c12c475fe067e74005e7dd83999bc3a19b478b15/src/sensors/softfusion/softfusionsensor.h#L109-L130). The normal [acceleration and rotation packet structures](https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP/blob/c12c475fe067e74005e7dd83999bc3a19b478b15/src/network/packets.h#L113-L157) contain values, sensor IDs, and rotation metadata, but **no sensor capture timestamp**. Packet sequence and host receive times cannot establish original sampling time or synchronized inter-tracker timing. They also cannot repair loss, batching, Wi-Fi delay, or clock drift.

`ENABLE_INSPECTION` is [false in the stock build](https://github.com/SlimeVR/SlimeVR-Tracker-ESP/blob/c12c475fe067e74005e7dd83999bc3a19b478b15/src/debug.h#L93-L97); no LSM6DSV SoftFusion call to the raw inspection sender was found in the pinned source. Normal packets do not provide raw synchronized gyro/accelerometer samples for re-running a raw-IMU filter. Custom firmware, build settings, and other IMUs may differ. No acceleration-based estimator is enabled on the strength of this source review; actual firmware, axes, sign, calibration, timing, and behavior still require hardware validation.
